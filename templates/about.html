About Me

Summary

I am a first-year Computer Science student at Warsaw University of Technology. Alongside my studies, I am currently expanding my knowledge of web development through the CS50x course. Programming is my passion, and I devote most of my free time to it. I bring a fresh perspective and a natural curiosity about new technologies, thanks to which I quickly absorb knowledge and am not afraid to ask questions where others might skip them. I enjoy learning new tools and technologies on my own, outside of any formal curriculum. I am characterized by an analytical approach to problem-solving and consistency - whatever I start, I always see through to the end. I am ready to fully commit my enthusiasm and energy to the growth of the team I work with, treating every task as an opportunity to learn and to make a real contribution, even at an early stage of my career. I see my first job in IT not just as a one-time experience, but as the beginning of a long-term path in the field, where I will be able to gain experience, learn from the team, and also actively support it with new ideas and a willingness to act.

Journey

My journey with computer science began very early - I was already fascinated by new technology as a child. My real programming story started at the beginning of high school, and I knew right away that this was the path I wanted to follow. While preparing for my matura exam in computer science, I spent countless hours programming in Python and working through tasks in Excel and Access. After finishing my matura, I started expanding my Python knowledge by building my first projects, and later began the CS50x course.
